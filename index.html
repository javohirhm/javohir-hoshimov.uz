Projects

  1. FootCoach · 2026

    Football analytics from one fixed camera. A GPU worker detects and tracks every player (RF-DETR + ByteTrack), maps them onto the pitch, and turns a match into distance, sprints, formations and space control. An AI analyst answers a coach's questions over the club's own data.

  2. xatolab · 2026

    IELTS practice that takes every wrong answer apart. 6,071 questions across 157 Listening and Reading tests: it grades, logs each mistake, finds the weakest task type and plans the next 20 days around it.

    Live

  3. HealthConnect · 2026

    Arrhythmia detection from a smartwatch, my graduation project. Galaxy Watch → Android app → FastAPI backend that reads live ECG and classifies each heartbeat into 5 classes. I compared SVM, Random Forest, XGBoost, 1D-CNN and CNN-LSTM on 109,438 MIT-BIH beats; the 1D-CNN reached 98.3% accuracy and 0.90 macro-F1.

    Code

  4. MedVision · 2026

    Breast ultrasound, built at a hackathon. EfficientNet-B0 classifies a scan as normal, benign or malignant; a U-Net with a ResNet-34 encoder outlines the tumour. Served as a FastAPI + React app with drag-and-drop upload and mask overlays.

    Code
